**No-edge / neutral** for a fresh swing entry (days-weeks). Intraday is a similar wash: below VWAP and opening range (bearish bias) but RSI(14,15m) 35.7 is close enough to oversold that a snap-back bounce is as likely as continuation. Not tradeable with conviction on either side right now — this is a "wait for resolution" call, not a directional one.

## Confidence

**3/10.** Not higher because trend (bearish) and momentum/volume (diverging bullish) directly conflict across both timeframes — a genuinely ambiguous read, not a masked directional lean. Not lower because the divergence (OBV up while price down, positive MACD histograms on both timeframes) is a real, data-grounded observation worth flagging for next week's re-check, not pure noise.
