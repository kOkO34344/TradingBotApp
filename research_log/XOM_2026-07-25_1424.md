# XOM research note — 2026-07-25 14:24

## Thesis

XOM is in a strong, volume-confirmed daily uptrend — price above all three moving averages (SMA20/50/200) and EMA21, MACD histogram bullish and expanding, OBV rising in step with price — capping a +46.6% 1-year run and +14.6% over the last 21 days alone. But the daily tape is extended: RSI(14) at 72.1 and price sitting at 106% of the upper Bollinger band (i.e., outside it) both flag overbought conditions, and the 63-day return (+4.9%) lagging the 21-day (+14.6%) shows the move has accelerated sharply just recently. The 15-minute picture is not confirming fresh upside urgency — price is pinned near its 15m EMA20, RSI is a neutral 49.8, and price sits inside the opening range and just below session VWAP (157.11), i.e., consolidating rather than breaking out intraday. Fundamentally, the trailing P/E (26.38) looks rich for an integrated oil & gas name with only 2.6% revenue growth and 7.8% margins, though the much lower forward P/E (14.62) implies the market is pricing a real earnings recovery. Net: a genuine daily uptrend that is due to digest recent gains before it can be re-entered with a favorable reward/risk.

## Direction & timeframe

**Long bias, swing (days-weeks) — but no-edge for a fresh entry at 156.89 today.** The daily trend and volume are unambiguously bullish; the entry decision should wait for the overbought condition to unwind rather than chase into resistance.

## Confidence

**5/10.** Not higher: RSI 72.1 plus price outside the upper Bollinger band on the daily is a classic overextension signature, and the 15m tape shows no confirming intraday thrust (flat RSI, below VWAP) to justify chasing here. Not lower: trend structure, MACD expansion, and OBV are all genuinely aligned bullish with no bearish divergence present in the data — this is a "wait for a better price," not a "fade the trend," setup.

## Key levels

- **Entry zone:** 154.91–155.68 — the prior swing resistance (154.91, now likely first support on a pullback) through the opening-range low (155.68). A pullback/consolidation into this zone that holds would offer a better-defined long entry than chasing at 156.89.
- **Invalidation/stop:** Below 144.71 (next daily swing support), or tighter at ~152.5 (entry-zone low minus ~1x daily ATR of 3.43) for a shorter-leash swing trade. Daily ATR of 3.43 (2.2% of price) sets realistic stop distance — a stop inside the current day's noise (under ~1 ATR) risks premature exit given 15m ATR is only 0.368.
- **Target:** 163.68 (next swing resistance); stretch target 175.22 (52-week high, currently 75% of the 52-week range away from the low).

## Risks

1. **Mean-reversion snapback:** RSI 72.1 and price outside the upper daily Bollinger band are conditions that have historically preceded multi-day pullbacks even within intact uptrends; the 21d return (+14.6%) is a lot to have banked in three weeks.
2. **Valuation gap dependent on delivery:** trailing P/E of 26.38 vs. forward P/E of 14.62 only makes sense if XOM materially grows earnings from here; revenue growth is presently just 2.6%, so if that acceleration doesn't show up, the multiple has room to compress.
3. **Intraday non-confirmation:** price below session VWAP and pinned inside the opening range on the 15m chart means there's no intraday momentum evidence yet that buyers are pushing through recent highs — a break of the 15m EMA20/VWAP level to the downside first would argue against entering long into strength.

## What would change my mind

- A daily RSI cooling back toward the 50-60 zone while price holds above the 144.71–154.91 support band would confirm a healthy digestion and turn the long entry from "wait" to "actionable."
- A 15-minute close back above session VWAP (157.11) with rising OBV/volume would be the first intraday confirmation that the daily trend is reasserting rather than stalling.
- A daily close back inside the Bollinger bands (below ~155.37) without breaking 154.91 support would still be constructive; a daily close below 144.71 would break trend structure and invalidate the long thesis entirely.
- Any forward-quarter earnings print that fails to close the trailing/forward P/E gap would undercut the valuation justification for chasing this extension.
