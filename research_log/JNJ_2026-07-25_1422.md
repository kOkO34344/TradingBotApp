JNJ is in a well-established daily uptrend — price is above SMA20/SMA50/SMA200/EMA21, and OBV's +47.7M 20-bar change confirms the advance is volume-backed rather than a low-participation drift. However, momentum is decelerating on both timeframes simultaneously: the daily MACD histogram is negative and contracting (-0.916) despite the MACD line still sitting above signal, and the 15-minute histogram is also negative (-0.1335) with RSI(15m) at 67 pushing toward overbought. Intraday structure is constructive (price above VWAP, above the opening range), so this reads as trend-intact-but-stretched rather than any reversal signal — the setup favors patience over chasing here.

## Direction & timeframe

No-edge for a *new* entry at current price (259.27); existing long exposure is still supported by trend/volume. If forced to characterize bias, it's long-biased on the swing (days-weeks) timeframe contingent on a better entry, not a chase.

## Confidence

4/10 — not higher because momentum is visibly contracting on both the daily and 15-minute MACD histograms while price is compressed against 52-week-high resistance, which is a poor risk/reward zone for initiating; not lower because trend structure (SMA/EMA stack) and OBV are unambiguously still bullish and nothing here signals reversal, only deceleration.

## Key levels

- **Entry zone (if this were being sized):** a pullback into 251.73-255.55 (EMA21/SMA20 confluence), ideally with RSI(14) cooling from 59.5 toward the mid-40s and MACD histogram flattening — not the current level.
- **Invalidation:** a daily close below 240.91 (SMA50, coincides with prior resistance-turned-support at 241.60) would break the intermediate structure.
- **Target:** 269.43 (52-week high and nearest swing resistance) — only ~4% away, which is itself why current entry risk/reward is unattractive.

## Risks

1. **Momentum/price divergence:** MACD histogram contracting on both daily and 15m while price is near the 52-week high — a classic setup for a multi-day consolidation or pullback even without a trend break.
2. **Resistance proximity:** 269.43 is both the 52-week high and nearest swing resistance, only 3.9% above spot — a poor place to initiate new risk since the reward-to-stop ratio using the 6.38 daily ATR (2.5% of price) is compressed.

## What would change my mind

- Daily MACD histogram turning back positive (deceleration reversing) while OBV keeps trending up — would support chasing strength toward/through 269.43.
- A clean daily close above 269.43 on expanding volume/OBV — would confirm breakout continuation rather than exhaustion.
- A pullback that holds 251.73-255.55 (EMA21/SMA20) with RSI resetting below 50 and MACD histogram flattening — would flip this into an actual entry setup rather than a "wait" call.
- A daily close below 240.91 (SMA50) — would invalidate the intermediate uptrend structure entirely and this would need to be reassessed as a trend-change, not just a momentum pause.
