## Risks

1. **Squeeze snap-back risk:** Bollinger-inside-Keltner compression historically precedes a sharp volatility expansion; a stop placed at a "normal" ATR distance from either trigger level above could still get tagged by the initial whip before the real move confirms.
2. **Divergence may not resolve soon:** OBV rising against a falling/flat price is a real signal but can persist for weeks before price catches up (or never resolves if it's driven by a handful of large-volume days rather than sustained accumulation) — don't treat it as a timing trigger by itself.
3. **Valuation offers no margin of safety:** trailing P/E 27.76 / forward 23.43 is a premium multiple for a 12.2%-margin, 16.6%-revenue-growth Internet Retail name — leaves little room for a fundamental disappointment (e.g., earnings) to compound a technical breakdown; nothing in this data set flags an upcoming catalyst, but the multiple itself is a risk amplifier, not a mitigant.
