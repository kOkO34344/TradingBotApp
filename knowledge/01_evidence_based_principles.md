# Evidence-based principles (verified sources only)

Distilled from sources with published data or audited records. No influencer claims.

## Base rates (the starting point for every decision)

- Academic brokerage-data studies consistently find ~97% of persistent retail day
  traders lose money net of costs (Chague et al., Brazilian futures market;
  Barber & Odean, US/Taiwan equity data). Any intraday signal must overcome this prior.
- Most backtested strategies fail live due to overfitting. A strategy tuned until the
  backtest looks good has near-zero expected out-of-sample edge.
- Buy-and-hold SPY is the benchmark every active idea must beat AFTER costs.
  In our own out-of-sample tests (2019-2026, 10 large caps): SMA crossovers,
  Donchian breakouts, and RSI-2 mean reversion ALL lost to buy-and-hold.

## What has published evidence of edge

- Cross-sectional momentum (Jegadeesh & Titman 1993 and hundreds of replications):
  buy recent 6-12 month winners, rebalance monthly. The most robust documented anomaly.
  Our own test: top-3-of-10 monthly rotation ~matched SPY with one-third less drawdown.
- Dual momentum (Antonacci): add an absolute-momentum filter — go to cash when
  trailing returns are negative. Documented to cut bear-market drawdowns.
- Opening Range Breakout (Zarattini & Aziz, SSRN 2023): 5-min ORB on QQQ 2016-2023,
  ~33% annualized alpha reported. BUT our faithful replication lost 12.6% over a recent
  60-trading-day window — 23% win rate, one 10R target hit. Lesson: even published
  edges are regime-dependent and have brutal losing stretches.

## What has NO verifiable evidence

- ICT / Smart Money Concepts (order blocks, fair value gaps, liquidity sweeps as
  taught by influencers): no audited accounts, no peer-reviewed backtests. Concepts
  loose enough to explain any outcome in hindsight = unfalsifiable = not evidence.
- Chart-pattern trading (head & shoulders, flags) as a standalone edge: repeated
  academic tests find no reliable out-of-sample profitability net of costs.

## Risk rules that all audited traders share (Grittani, Dux, Cameron)

- Fixed-fractional sizing: risk a set % of equity per trade (1-2%), never "conviction sizing" upward.
- Hard stop known BEFORE entry; position size derived from stop distance, not the reverse.
- Daily loss limit that halts trading — enforced mechanically, not by willpower.
- Their edges were niche + discretionary (low-float small caps, tape reading).
  Their risk discipline is the transferable part; their entries are not.

## Calibration rules for this agent's own notes

- A confidence-7 call should be right clearly more often than a confidence-4 call;
  if grading shows otherwise, confidence numbers are noise and must be recalibrated.
- "No edge here" is a valid, creditable call. Forced directional calls destroy calibration.
- Never cite a price level not present in the provided data.
