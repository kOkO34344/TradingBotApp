## Risks

1. **Squeeze resolves violently and gaps past both conditional levels** — Bollinger-inside-Keltner compressions often release with an expansion candle that skips the "clean" entry zone entirely; chasing after the fact means a worse risk/reward than the levels above imply.

3. **Macro/sector risk not in the technicals** — PG is a bond-proxy staples name with real FX exposure (large multinational revenue base); rate moves or dollar strength can override the chart independent of anything shown here.

## What would change my mind
